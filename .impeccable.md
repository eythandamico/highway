## Design Context

### Users and use cases
This is a reusable starter for developers creating independent applications. Its neutral demo demonstrates sign-in, protected workspaces, records, settings and private files. The next product's users and domain are intentionally unspecified; establish them with its owner before building product-specific screens.

### Brand personality
Practical, clear and approachable. Preserve the shared foundation, readable hierarchy and calm interactions. The requested Yardwork consistency refresh supplies lime/forest actions, warm raised surfaces and useful progressive blurs as defaults.

### Principles
- Rebrand through semantic tokens; keep status colors independent.
- One owner for typography, controls, overlays and surfaces; no feature-level forks.
- Forms preserve drafts, block duplicate actions and report truthful results.
- Mobile, keyboard, enlarged text, reduced motion and dark mode are first-class states.
- This template carries no Yardwork customers, artwork, credentials, production identifiers or domain backend.

### Approved demo and documentation direction
The owner explicitly requested the original Yardwork demo look and interactions, isolated in this personal repository and Cloudflare account. The audience is developers exploring and reusing this foundation. Preserve the Yardwork office screens and synthetic fixtures as a demo; provide an Invariant-inspired searchable showcase with live controls, usage code, and a practical how-to guide. Keep the lime/forest palette, warm materials, existing typography, and restrained motion.

### Invariant collection
The owner requested Invariant's actual components and its meticulous execution, motion, and design engineering. Preserve the original component materials and interaction details in a distinct reusable collection. Use its measured indicators, coherent timing, layered elevation and restrained feedback as references when extending the Foundation library. Keep the existing Yardwork demo appearance intact.
