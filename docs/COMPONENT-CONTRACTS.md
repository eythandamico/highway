## display

Preserve readable content, semantics and host layout; decorative content must not intercept input.

Required review states: default, long-content, light-dark, zoom, narrow.

## action

Use native activation, accessible names, visible focus and explicit button type. Host owns success and side effects.

Required review states: default, hover, focus, pressed, disabled, pending, failure.

## field

Keep labels, refs, native names/autofill and event contracts. Do not silently switch controlledness.

Required review states: default, focus, disabled, invalid, long-content, controlled, uncontrolled-when-supported.

## overlay

Restore focus, scope portals, coordinate Escape/outside dismissal and make closed content non-interactive. Do not bypass an outer focus trap.

Required review states: closed, open, nested, pending-when-supported, rapid-reversal, reduced-motion.

## selection

Reflect controlled values truthfully. Keyboard input is immediate; preserve pointer motion and resize alignment.

Required review states: default, selected, keyboard, pointer, disabled-when-supported, resize, reduced-motion.

## feedback

Use truthful labels; retain host data/drafts on failure. Cancel owned timers and animation frames on unmount.

Required review states: loading, success, failure, empty-when-supported, long-content, reduced-motion.

## layout

Use semantic spacing and allow content growth; avoid layout changes outside the adopted scope.

Required review states: default, narrow, long-content, zoom.

## effect

Keep decorative layers bounded and pointer-transparent. Preserve artwork and readable foreground content.

Required review states: default, light-dark, reduced-motion, reduced-transparency, narrow.

## helper

Keep helpers side-effect free during render and do not introduce application infrastructure.

Required review states: server-render, nested-scope-when-supported.

## Motion: none

Immediate content and keyboard feedback.

Do not add animation without a communication purpose.

Owner: `packages/highway-styles/src/tokens.css`.

## Motion: press

Tactile confirmation for pointer activation.

Preserve existing press geometry and focus. Reduced motion removes spatial feedback.

Owner: `packages/highway-styles/src/controls.css`.

## Motion: overlay

Anchor a transient surface to its trigger.

Preserve component-owned entrance/exit recipes: Invariant scale/slide enters at 300ms with 200ms opacity, exits at 150ms with 120ms opacity. Keep correct origins, retargetable transitions, reduced motion, and inert closed content.

Owner: `packages/highway-react/src/invariant/lib/popover.js`.

## Motion: selection

Connect pointer selection to the active indicator.

Preserve each selection recipe: Tabs uses its 300ms emphasized travel; SegmentedControl retains 250ms travel. Keyboard and reduced-motion paths are immediate; measurements respond to resizing and wrapping.

Owner: `packages/highway-react/src/lib/motion.js`.

## Motion: slider

Quick pointer clicks settle with the original spring; sustained dragging tracks directly.

250ms spring, 80ms drag threshold; keyboard and reduced motion instant.

Owner: `packages/highway-react/src/lib/motion.js`.

## Motion: expansion

Reveal related content without abruptly changing its container.

350ms height, 250ms content with 100ms entrance delay, 150ms content exit; interruptions retarget; hidden content inert.

Owner: `packages/highway-react/src/lib/motion.js`.

## Motion: inherited-specialty

Preserve a distinct existing choreography until individually reviewed.

Do not normalize all timings. Any change requires explicit visual comparison, reduced-motion coverage and lifecycle cleanup.

Owner: `packages/highway-react/src/invariant/components`.
